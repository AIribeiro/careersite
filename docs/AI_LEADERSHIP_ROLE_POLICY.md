# AI leadership role policy (20 October 2026)

Primary ambition: AI Director, Director of AI Transformation, and equivalent enterprise Head of AI mandates.

Valid conversion targets: Senior Manager with ownership of enterprise AI strategy, AI Strategy Lead, AI Transformation Lead, Head of AI Adoption, and equivalent functional AI leadership roles. These are not fallback or lower-quality roles. Assess accountability for roadmaps, portfolio, governance, adoption, operating model, stakeholders and business value rather than managerial title alone.

Adjacent but secondary: Data and Analytics leadership, responsible AI, governance, and enterprise automation leadership with relevant decision rights. Exclude primarily hands-on engineering, ML/DS, engineering architecture, PMO, quota sales, implementation-only delivery, or generic IT management.

The market denominator must distinguish primary ambition from conversion-eligible mandates; do not equate title matching with validated suitability. Geographical relevance covers Sweden, with Göteborg preferred and Stockholm acceptable.

Model impact: Do not raise offer probability or accelerate dates based only on a larger discovery basket. Keep stage-confirmed interviews and actual offers as strongest personal evidence. Separate the versioned role-basket comparison from the original JobTech v1 time series; v1 cannot be restated or interpreted as a census of matching senior roles.
