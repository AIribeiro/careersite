# WEEKLY AI — Social visual identity (canonical from Week 42 · 2026)

## Purpose and scope
This is the **standing art-direction contract** for any future user-requested WEEKLY AI social-sharing image in Jair Ribeiro's AI & Data Portfolio. The reference is the approved/generated Week 42 image, **“Adoption, Agents and Human Judgment”** (cover dated 12–18 October 2026). Recreate its *visual language*, not its exact scene or headline. This guide applies to image generation when explicitly requested; it does **not** silently authorize automatic image generation or social posting, and does not change the Monday editorial/audio workflow, CMS, analytics or Tue–Sat text-only social schedule.

## Visual language — mandatory
- **Genre and audience:** Premium, credible management-editorial cover; considered enterprise AI/data leadership perspective. Photorealistic, quietly cinematic, human-centered and business-literate, rather than futuristic AI advertising.
- **Color:** Warm ivory / off-white as the headline field; deep midnight/navy typography, charcoal details, muted copper/bronze for slim rules, labels, accents and icon strokes. Warm natural wood plus subdued blue-greys in photography. High readable contrast. Avoid neon, saturated gradients, red/green dashboard cards and glossy techno-hype.
- **Typography:** High-contrast editorial serif for one dominant left-aligned headline; clean restrained sans-serif for supporting copy. Small uppercase letterspaced **WEEKLY AI · WEEK N · ISO YEAR** identifier in copper, with a short copper horizontal rule below. Oversized navy headline in 2–4 carefully balanced lines. Supporting factual subtitle in understated navy sans serif. Never put social-post copy into the image.
- **Signature layout:** A wide-near-square 6:5 or similarly balanced social-cover composition. Editorial text has generous negative space in the **left 52–57%**, laptop/workspace still life anchors the **right 43–48%**, and desk objects provide grounded depth across the bottom. Avoid text/image collisions. A hero MacBook with plausible keyboard/screen is visible, slightly angled. Keep the human physical workspace and the digital subject in one coherent frame.
- **Scene and light:** Soft golden-hour/daylit contemporary desk near a window, with a softly defocused waterfront, Nordic cityscape or neutral architectural setting. Shallow depth-of-field; warm highlights; controlled soft shadows. A notebook with pen, modest coffee mug bearing a subtle JR/Jair Ribeiro mark, and optionally a small plant are restrained recurring visual cues. No portrait or prominent face; never assume visual likeness.
- **Laptop content:** Dark navy branded “Weekly AI” digital cover / journal page with a restrained abstract illustration relevant to the issue (e.g. connected planet, enterprise workflow, governance/decision pathways, industrial system). Small “Jair Ribeiro”/site-brand treatment is allowed. Laptop visuals are illustrative: do not impersonate a real website screenshot or invent precise source statistics in the screen art.
- **Topic markers:** Optional narrow horizontal row of three to five consistent simple copper line icons, each with a short uppercase factual theme label derived from the verified edition. For the Week 42 reference: ADOPTION · AGENTS · OPERATIONS · GOVERNANCE · HUMAN JUDGMENT. These are reference examples, **not mandatory labels for later weeks**. Labels/icons must actually reflect that week's stories; omit rather than force if overcrowded.
- **Signature/footer:** Discreet lower-left **JR | Jair Ribeiro** and **AI & DATA LEADERSHIP**; optional small lower-right “WEEKLY AI / A CURATED BRIEFING ON ENTERPRISE AI” with a copper rule. Keep logo/wordmark subtle, never larger than the edition title.
- **Mood:** Quiet authority, reflective practicality, accessible warmth, executive publishing design. Intent: invite a professional human reader to think, not sell a product or make a grand claim.

## Information contract (dynamic per edition)
Before art generation, retrieve **actual published** Weekly AI CMS issue and validate:
1. Headline without redundant suffix (do not repeat “Week N · YYYY” at the end of a headline when the identifier is separately displayed).
2. Correct **covered ISO week / ISO week-year**, Monday–Sunday date range, **not** merely the publication date. At year boundary use the covered ISO week-year.
3. Concise subtitle such as “Five enterprise AI developments from 12–18 October 2026.” Use the actual story count (3–5) and dates.
4. Three to five topic markers selected from that issue's verified source-backed themes. Do not invent a news item, publisher claim, figure or quote.
5. Small signature brand only. A social image must **not** contain links, URLs, QR codes, hashtags, post text, supposed endorsements, faux publisher logos, audio controls, or invented metrics. Social copy and source-linked, attributed short URLs are separate chat deliverables.

## Variation rule — brand consistency without repeated scene
Preserve the color, headline typography, week-label treatment, dominant composition, natural light, laptop, and restrained JR footer each week. Intentionally vary **at least three** topic-specific details across editions: (a) desk arrangement/accessory type, (b) time of day/view/background setting, (c) laptop screen concept and its visual metaphor, (d) the icon themes/order, (e) camera framing/angle. Avoid identical globe art, layout of notebook/mug/plant, or identical skyline every week. Differences should be editorially meaningful, not decoration. Do not use unrequested people/faces.

## Responsive and legibility quality gate
- Master artwork close to **6:5 / 1200×1000** (reference near this ratio), with text-safe margins of about 5% on all sides. If preparing a separate 16:9 or 1.91:1 platform crop, **reflow** the composition rather than cropping out headline or brand; do not silently reformat without need.
- Readable at mobile feed size; the headline is the one dominant element, subordinate copy no more than two short lines where practical. Leave enough uncluttered space for typography.
- Check **every** rendered word, date, week numeral, accent, publisher reference, monogram, laptop logo, icon label and spelling. Correct uncanny distorted typography/hands/objects, malformed laptop geometry and broken faces (prefer zero people). Never approve a cover with an incorrect date, duplicate title, bad spacing or clipped text.
- Show only the requested image in the image-generation result; compose platform-native social-sharing texts separately in chat, with verified first-party short links, campaign attribution and original source credit. Images do not contain social text.
- This spec is art direction, **not proof** that an image was generated, uploaded to the website, or published to social networks. Report each state truthfully.
