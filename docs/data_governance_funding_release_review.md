## Reader decision

A senior leader should decide whether to fund a bounded corrective control, fund diagnosis of the underlying issue, or withhold a larger rollout. The central argument is that business exposure and verified operating improvement—not a generic governance maturity ambition—should determine discretionary investment. Required legal, security, contractual or unacceptable-risk controls form a distinct path and still need right-sized scope and accountable delivery.

## Original contribution

**Exposure → Cause → Control → Proof** is an independently written management decision bridge. The catalog preview abbreviates this as Exposure → Control → Proof. It does not copy the supplied third-party five-section business-case infographic or its icons/graphic arrangement.
