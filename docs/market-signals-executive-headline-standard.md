# Market Signals — executive headline standard

Effective 17 November 2026. Applies to the Market Signals landing page,
historical public sample and authenticated Observatory.

Headlines must read like the table of contents of a serious executive
research publication, not promotional copy.

## Editorial rules

- **Name the measure.** Prefer “Enterprise AI adoption” to “AI is everywhere.”
  Prefer “Selected AI & Data leadership advertisements” to general claims
  about AI recruitment or a talent shortage.
- **State scope when it changes meaning.** Specify Sweden, the observed time
  window and the original population. A selective title-basket is not the
  entire national AI labour market.
- **Distinguish fact from interpretation.** SCB measures reported AI use,
  not financial return. Brainville reports consulting marketplace activity
  across professions, not AI-specialist supply. JobTech captures adverts,
  not filled positions.
- **Describe value as a practical research use.** Compare complete periods,
  inspect source definitions, question assumptions, prepare a leadership
  discussion or download a published evidence record. Do not promise
  company-specific decisions or an automatic market verdict.
- **Keep the edition visible.** Edition 01 is dated November 2026;
  June–October archive views have only retrospective JobTech evidence.
- **Describe any missing source data where it is listed.** Skills,
  management-pay and several sector indicators remain under review. Eight
  sections are not eight complete datasets.
- **Use neutral access language.** “Access the published research” is
  preferable to “try it”, “unlock”, “discover” or countdown-style sales copy.
- **Use executive judgement, not marketing idioms.** The preferred
  vocabulary is evidence, observation, implication, condition, limitation,
  decision, risk, capability and source. Avoid exaggeration, rhetorical
  hooks and claims of certainty that cannot be reproduced.

## Release checks

Every change to a published headline should keep the following intact:
the 30 approved Edition 01 observations and four analyses; the three
source populations; member-only full reports; no-login five-page historical
sample; working download routes; immutable archive boundaries; original
publisher references and the statement of uncertainty.

The release tests check the headline register and compare the landing
claims with the actual dashboards and report generators. The broader site
suite may contain unrelated failures; do not imply complete deployment
verification from a dedicated test alone.
