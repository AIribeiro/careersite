Welcome to Weekly A I, the Friday perspective from my A I and Data Portfolio.

This week, four developments point to the same leadership challenge: the technology is moving faster than the decisions needed to make it dependable.

Gartner says seventy-one percent of surveyed organizations expect to deploy agentic A I within a year, but only twenty-one percent report having agentic governance capabilities today. That's a warning about readiness, not proof that those deployments will happen.

Open A I introduced Businesses in ChatGPT, allowing eligible companies to manage their information and, in some cases, offer business agents. When A I becomes a customer touchpoint, who owns the accuracy of what it says?

Reuters reports that Google introduced new A I chips, including a version designed to protect sensitive data in regulated industries.

And Reuters describes how Federal Reserve officials are weighing A I's potential productivity gains against infrastructure costs and disruption to jobs.

These are different stories, but they meet in one place: accountability.

My takeaway is to make the business owner, information owner and decision rights explicit before expanding autonomy. Then measure what changes for customers and employees, not simply how much A I the organization has bought.
