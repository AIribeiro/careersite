# Swedish Job Market → AI & Data Market Signals: editorial crosswalk

Last mapped: 16 November 2026. Change control: source-reviewed publications only. Owner: Jair Ribeiro.

## Source separation
The private Swedish Job Market Analytics workspace is a general Swedish job-market and individual career intelligence application. It combines external market records with personal applications, recruiter stages, portfolio behaviour, and shadow employment timing. The public Advisory service is an **enterprise AI/Data market intelligence product**, not a clone of that page. It exclusively reads the curated `portfolio_market_signal_points` and `portfolio_market_signal_briefs` tables using a verified member session.

## Incorporated analytics views
1. **What is changing? → Executive market pulse:** factual AI adoption, title-basket leadership adverts, in-house analytics, marketplace assignment and consultant supply context. No invented composite AI market score.
2. **Market trends at a glance → Comparable indicator explorer:** each series remains in its original units, region, vintage and source. At least two comparable complete periods are required before displaying a trend line. Stale and incomplete observations are separate.
3. **Regional market signal → Leadership demand dashboard:** original frozen JobTech v1 monthly title basket for Sweden, Västra Götaland and Stockholm; complete versus incomplete months and retrospective corrections remain separate. National and regional counts are nested and may not be summed.
4. **AI adoption beyond IT → Enterprise adoption & capabilities:** SCB adoption by firm size, Data/Analytics and BI capability; barriers among businesses considering but not adopting AI separated from adopters' denominators.
5. **Market intent vs actual hiring → Consulting & sector conditions:** Q4 employer outlook by IT, services, VGR and adjacent sectors (Manpower); KI services expectations; Almega ICT output/employment divergence; Swedbank service PMI; Brainville supply and remote-work context. All require publisher sign-off before public visibility.
6. **AI occupational diffusion → Talent & management economics:** TechSverige AI mentions beyond IT and occupational breadth; SCB 2025 private-sector IT and R&D manager salaries as general wage proxies, never AI Director salary estimates.
7. **Contradictory signals → Cross-source interpretations:** inspect each input and limits separately. Two publishers do not prove causality or independent replication.
8. **Sources & measurement → Curated source ledger:** filter by lens/geography/publisher, inspect population/source/date/caveat and export a source-linked CSV.

## Excluded from the public service
- Personal job applications, interviews, recruiter responses, rejections, employer identities or offer-date forecasts.
- Portfolio traffic versus personal recruiting associations, raw analytics events or individual visitor patterns.
- National GDP, broad unemployment or general workforce counts unless a specific executive AI/Data interpretation later justifies one as context.
- Business confidence as an invented measure of AI vacancies, raw IT job postings as AI director roles, and one-off cross-sectional observations treated as sustained trends.

## Measurement guardrails
- Always display the observation period, publisher publication date, verification date, methodology and denominator.
- Comparisons of unlike quantities use separate panels and editorial text, not a shared numerical axis or ratio.
- SCB 2026 enterprise AI coverage may differ from older years because sector and classification definitions changed.
- TechSverige AI advertisement keyword mentions and occupational counts are not unique vacancies.
- Manpower net employment outlook is the difference between employers expecting increases and decreases, not the probability of an individual hire.
- Almega ICT output growth and employment decline do not establish AI job displacement.
- Brainville data encompass all consulting disciplines, not AI/Data consulting specialists.
- SCB IT/R&D management wages are occupation-wide proxies, not validated AI or Data leadership salary ranges.

## Release process
Open Advisory Operations → Service management → Market Signals management → Analytics coverage & intake. Confirm the source families and draft set. Then review each original publisher in Source assurance and curate evidence in Evidence catalogue. Once reviewed interpretations and a complete report manifest are approved, record an editorial edition. The existing release gate rejects missing/unpublished/stale evidence, incompatible PDF inputs and unsupported cross-source claims.

## Not an automatic public mirror
The source registry refresh can ingest new upstream releases. Those observations do not automatically appear in the Advisory market dashboards. A public publication step requires explicit provenance, review, versioning and an auditable release decision.
